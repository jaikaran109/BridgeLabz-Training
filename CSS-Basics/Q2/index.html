The City of the Taj Mahal

Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India, famous for its magnificent monuments, rich culture, and Mughal heritage.

History of Agra

Agra has played an important role in Indian history. It became a major center during the Mughal period and is home to several famous historical monuments.

Famous Places

Taj Mahal

A world-famous monument built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.

Agra Fort

A historic fort known for its impressive Mughal architecture.

Fatehpur Sikri

A historic city near Agra featuring remarkable Mughal architecture.

Culture of Agra

Agra is known for its traditional handicrafts, Mughlai food, marble artwork, leather products, and vibrant local markets.
